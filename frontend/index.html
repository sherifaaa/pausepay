    Frequently asked questions

    Is PausePay safe to use? +

    Yes. PausePay uses Plaid, the same technology trusted by Venmo, Robinhood, and Coinbase. We never see or store your bank password — Plaid provides read-only access to your transaction data.

    Can PausePay access my money? +

    No. We have read-only access. We can see your transactions, but we cannot move, withdraw, or transfer any funds.

    How much can I save? +

    The average American wastes $512 per year on forgotten subscriptions. Many PausePay users save $200+ per year within their first month.

    Can I cancel anytime? +

    Yes. You can cancel your PausePay subscription at any time with one click. No hidden fees, no contracts, no hassle.

    Which countries do you support? +

    PausePay works in the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Ireland, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Netherlands, Belgium, Portugal, Austria, and Finland.
