      Frequently asked questions

      Is PausePay safe to use? +

      Yes. PausePay uses Plaid, trusted by Venmo, Robinhood, and Coinbase. We never see or store your bank password — Plaid provides read-only access to your transaction data.

      Do you sell my data? +

      Never. We do not sell, share, or rent your data to advertisers, brokers, or any third party. Your transaction history is used for one purpose only: finding subscriptions you can cancel.

      Do you train AI on my data? +

      No. Your transactions are never used to train any AI model — ours or anyone else's.

      Can PausePay access my money? +

      No. We have read-only access. We can see your transactions, but we cannot move, withdraw, or transfer any funds.

      How does the business product work? +

      Businesses connect corporate cards. PausePay detects every recurring tool subscription, flags duplicates, and generates cost recovery invoices you can rebill to clients. Companies recover $12,000–$50,000 per year in wasted software spend.

      Which countries do you support? +

      United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Ireland, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Netherlands, Belgium, Portugal, Austria, and Finland.
